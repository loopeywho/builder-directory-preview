Editorial and commercial policy

Listings carry individual evidence labels: researched, new/unranked, or hands-on tested. New/unranked entries are not recommendations or ranked winners. Test evidence in operating reports applies only to this local directory software unless a vendor evaluation is explicitly documented. Future paid featured placements will be labelled Sponsored and kept separate from editorial order. Affiliate links require adjacent disclosure; none are used here. Newsletter sponsorship remains a later option. No accounts, forms, customer data intake, payments or analytics.

Research coverage and access gaps are recorded in dated operating reports. Listing checked dates describe source review, not vendor execution. Social snippets and maker claims are not independent validation.
